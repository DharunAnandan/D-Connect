# D-Connect privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

D-Connect links your Android phone with your own computer (Linux or Windows). This policy explains what the D-Connect apps access and where it goes.

## The short version

- D-Connect has **no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics**.
- Everything the phone app reads goes **only to the computer you paired it with**. It travels over an encrypted connection (TLS, pinned to that computer's certificate) on your own Wi-Fi network or USB cable.
- Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## What the phone app accesses, and why

Each item is used only for the feature named. You can deny any permission; that feature then stays off.

| Data / permission | Used for | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications (notification access) | Showing your phone's notifications on your PC, replying, dismissing | Your paired PC only |
| SMS (read, receive, send) | Reading and sending text messages from your PC | Your paired PC only; messages you send go out through your phone's SIM |
| Phone state, call log, answer calls, make calls | Showing incoming calls on the PC, answering, declining or dialling from the PC, recent calls | Your paired PC only |
| Contacts | Showing names instead of numbers | Your paired PC only |
| Photos and videos | Browsing your gallery from the PC, copying files you choose | Your paired PC only |
| Camera | Scanning the pairing QR code on your PC's screen | Stays on the phone; no images are stored or sent |
| Bluetooth | Letting your PC act as a hands-free device so call audio plays on the PC | Bluetooth link between your phone and PC |
| Accessibility service | Letting the PC control the phone while you mirror its screen, and pressing the call screen's buttons when you answer from the PC. It is used only when you start these from your PC. | Taps and gestures come from your paired PC; screen content is not collected |
| Screen capture (media projection) | Mirroring your phone's screen to your PC, only after you approve the system prompt each time | Your paired PC only |
| Clipboard | Shared copy and paste between phone and PC, when turned on | Your paired PC only |
| Files you share | Sending files between phone and PC | Your paired PC only |
| Do Not Disturb access | Turning focus mode on or off from the PC | Stays on the phone |
| Battery, storage, network status | Showing the phone's status on the PC | Your paired PC only |

## What is stored

- **On the phone:** the pairing details for your PC (its address, a key and its certificate fingerprint) and your settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them. App backup is turned off, so they are never copied to a cloud backup.
- **On the PC:** your settings, the pairing key for your phone and eye-break statistics. They are stored in your user profile and readable only by your account. Messages, notifications and clipboard history are kept in memory while the app runs and are not written to disk. Files you send from the phone are saved in the folder you chose (Downloads by default).

## Internet use

- Pairing and all of the features above work on your local network or USB. They do not go through the internet.
- To check for updates, both apps download a small public file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). The request contains no personal data. Like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement).
- When you tap a link or an "Update" button, it opens in your browser or in Google Play.

## Children

D-Connect is not directed at children under 13.
