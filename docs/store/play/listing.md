```

**Short description** (80 max)
```
Calls, messages, notifications and files from your Android phone on your PC.
```

**Full description** (4000 max)
```
D-Connect puts your Android phone on your Linux or Windows computer, so you can stay on your PC and still keep up with your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Notifications: see your phone's notifications on your PC, reply inline and dismiss them
• Messages: read and send SMS from your keyboard
• Calls: see who's calling, answer or decline from the PC, dial from your PC, and take the call through your laptop's speakers and mic
• Files: send files both ways by drag and drop or the share sheet
• Clipboard: copy on one device, paste on the other
• Photos and videos: browse your gallery and albums from the PC
• Screen: mirror your phone's screen and control it with your mouse and keyboard
• Remote: use your phone as a touchpad, keyboard or presentation remote for the PC
• Media: control music on either device from the other
• Find my phone: ring your phone at full volume, even on silent
• PC actions: lock, sleep, mute, screenshot or record the PC from your phone
• Battery alerts for both devices
• Eye care: 20-20-20 breaks that follow you from screen to screen

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no servers, no ads, no tracking
• Your data goes only to the computer you pair with, over an encrypted connection on your own Wi-Fi or USB cable
• Pair once by scanning a QR code

HOW TO START
1. Install D-Connect on your computer (Ubuntu/Linux or Windows): github.com/DharunAnandan/D-Connect/releases
2. Open it and scan the QR code with this app
3. Allow only the features you want to use

D-Connect needs the D-Connect app on your computer to work.
```

```
D-Connect is a companion app for the D-Connect desktop app (Linux or Windows). To test:
1. Install the desktop app from https://github.com/DharunAnandan/D-Connect/releases/latest

2. Open it on a computer on the same Wi-Fi network as the phone. It shows a QR code.
3. In the phone app, tap "Scan QR code" and scan it. The phone and PC are now linked.
