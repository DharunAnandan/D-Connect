**Description** (Markdown):
```
D-Connect puts your Android phone on your Linux computer, so you can stay on your PC and still keep up with your phone.

**What you can do**

* **Notifications**: see your phone's notifications on your PC, reply inline and dismiss them
* **Messages**: read and send SMS from your keyboard
* **Calls**: see who's calling, answer or decline from the PC, and talk through your laptop's speakers and mic (Bluetooth hands-free, with AI noise removal)
* **Files**: send files both ways by drag and drop
* **Clipboard**: copy on one device, paste on the other
* **Photos and videos**: browse your phone's gallery and albums
* **Screen**: mirror your phone and control it with your mouse and keyboard
* **Remote**: use your phone as a touchpad, keyboard or presentation remote
* **Media**: control music on either device from the other
* **Find my phone**: ring your phone at full volume, even on silent
* **PC actions**: lock, sleep, mute, screenshot or record the PC from your phone
* **Battery alerts** for both devices, and **20-20-20 eye care** that follows you from screen to screen

**Private by design**: no accounts, no servers, no tracking. Your data goes only between your phone and your PC, over an encrypted connection on your own Wi-Fi or USB cable.

**Getting started**

1. Install the D-Connect app on your Android phone (Google Play, or the APK from the GitHub releases page)
2. Open D-Connect on your PC and scan the QR code with the phone app
3. Allow only the features you want to use
```
